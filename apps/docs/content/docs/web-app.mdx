title: Web app
description: The same engine in the browser, a scenario on the left and the plan as a graph or as text.
---

[The web app](https://simulci.vercel.app) plans your workflows in the browser. The engine runs in a Web Worker on
the files you open; they stay in the page.

- **Your workflows.** Open folder where the browser allows it (Reload reads it again), Choose folder (a copy),
  Paste YAML, drop files on the page, or a sample: Starter or Monorepo.
- **The scenario** on the left: the event, its ref or pull request, the changed files, inputs and the rest of a
  [scenario](/docs/reference/scenarios). The URL keeps it; Back and Forward move between events.
- **The plan** in the middle, as a graph or as text (`t`; below 1024 px wide, text only).
- **The panels** on the right: Reasons, Unknowns (with the assumptions you pinned above them), Node and Problems
  (`r`, `1`–`4`). Pin an unknown from its row and the plan follows.
- **The YAML pane** (`\`, or **Open the workflow source** under the plan): the files, with each line's verdict and the
  line that decided the selection. It is read-only until edit mode.
- **Copy as test case** (`c`): the plan of the focused workflow as a [test case](/docs/reference/test-files), to copy,
  download or [add to the workflow's test file](/docs/builder/test-cases).
- **Edit mode** (`e`, or the top bar's **Edit**): the [builder](/docs/builder). It opens the YAML pane and makes it
  editable, turns the Node tab into Properties and the scenario's event into the triggers editor, and lets you add,
  connect and remove jobs and steps on the canvas. Every edit is planned again as you make it, and Save writes it back
  to the folder.

`[` and `]` close and open the scenario column and the panels. The single-letter keys can be turned off from the top
bar's Shortcuts switch; [Keys](/docs/builder/keys) lists them all.
